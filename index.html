What is Apporatory?

Apporatory is a place to explore maths and science, from your very first sums to college-level ideas.

How it helps

Anyone can learn at their own level, at school or at home.

Teachers set up a whole class in minutes and find the right topic for every lesson.

Our goals

  • Make maths and science exciting, not scary.
  • Cover every grade from 1 to 12, and keep going into college.
  • Explain big ideas in short, clear steps.
  • Let everyone learn comfortably, whatever their eyesight, device or pace.
  • Help students and teachers learn together.
