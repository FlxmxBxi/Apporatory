Changes made for Apporatory
---------------------------
- Kept the 32-bar section from 0:15.743 to 2:02.410 of the original. In the
  original this section repeats sample for sample, so its end runs straight
  back into its start; a 1-second crossfade is baked into the head of the file
  as a safety margin, and the file plays on a loop.
- Lowered the level by 2.5 dB to about -21 LUFS so it sits quietly under the
  page, including on phones that ignore volume settings.
- Re-encoded from the original 320 kbps MP3 to a 160 kbps MP3 (about 2 MB).
