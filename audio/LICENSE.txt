Music in this folder
====================

The Sound panel picks a track at random from these four files and from
"Ambient chords", which is generated in the browser by js/sound.js (no
recording, nothing to license). All files retrieved 27 November 2026.

Changes made to every file: leading and trailing silence trimmed, the level
matched to about -24 LUFS so the tracks sit quietly under the page and at the
same loudness as each other, and re-encoded to MP3 at 80-112 kbps (2.4-2.9 MB).

Credit, as shown in the site footer and on the "coming soon" page:

  "Morning Coffee" by HoliznaCC0 is public domain (CC0).
